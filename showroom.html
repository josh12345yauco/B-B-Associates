The Experience

A Curated Space for
Discerning Homeowners

Most remodeling decisions are made from small digital thumbnails. We built our showroom to change that. Every material we carry is displayed at full scale — cabinets alongside countertops, tile alongside fixtures — so you experience the complete picture before committing.

Our design consultants guide you through each selection, explaining durability, maintenance requirements, and how each material performs in real homes. No pressure. No upsell. Just clarity.

Full-scale cabinet and countertop vignettes
Tile and stone displays with natural lighting
Plumbing fixtures and hardware from premium brands
Complimentary design consultation with every visit
Take-home samples to see materials in your own light
Cabinetry
Framed and frameless construction in painted, stained, and specialty finishes. Custom sizing available.
Countertops & Stone
Quartz, quartzite, marble, granite, and porcelain slabs. Edge profiles demonstrated at scale.
Tile & Flooring
Porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, and luxury vinyl tile in hundreds of patterns and colorways.
Plumbing & Fixtures
Faucets, sinks, showers, tubs, and hardware in polished, brushed, and matte finishes.
Lighting & Hardware
Pendant lighting, under-cabinet illumination, cabinet pulls, and decorative hardware collections.
