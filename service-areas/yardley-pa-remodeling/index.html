Kitchens · Yardley

Kitchen Remodeling
in Yardley, PA

B&B Associates Creations designs and builds kitchens in Yardley Borough and Lower Makefield Township, about 35 minutes from our Maple Glen office. We handle Borough and Township permits and price every line before work begins.

Lower Makefield: the center-hall colonial

Lower Makefield's developments from the 1970s through the 1990s share a kitchen: a galley or L-shape against the back wall, oak cabinets to a soffit, a peninsula that blocks the family room, and a breakfast area under a bay window. The remodel is almost always the same three moves: the soffit and peninsula come out, the wall to the family room opens where it is not bearing, and one island with seating takes the center of the room.

These houses have modern framing and plumbing, so the budget goes to cabinetry, stone, a real hood and lighting.

Yardley Borough: Victorians by the canal

The Borough's Victorian and early-1900s homes near the canal and Main Street have kitchens at the back of the house that were added or enlarged more than once, with a step-down floor and a mix of plaster and drywall. The first decision is whether the rear wall can open to the yard; the second is where the range goes once the old chimney is accounted for. We open a test area before quoting so wiring, plumbing and joists are known lines, not surprises.

Inset cabinetry, a farmhouse sink under the rear window, honed stone and warm metal suit these houses. We build the sample doors and set them in the kitchen before you decide.

Bathrooms · Yardley

Bathroom Remodeling
in Yardley, PA

B&B Associates Creations remodels bathrooms in Yardley Borough and Lower Makefield Township, about 35 minutes from our Maple Glen office. Our own crews set every tile surface, and we file with the Borough or Township and coordinate every inspection.

The Lower Makefield primary bath

Lower Makefield's colonials came with a primary bath the builder squeezed beside a walk-in closet: a fiberglass tub-shower, a small double vanity, and a door that hits the toilet. The remodel we do most often here moves the wall, takes some of the closet, and turns the space into a walk-in tile shower with frameless glass, a freestanding tub where there is room, a custom vanity with real storage and heated floors under large-format porcelain.

Moving plumbing in a second-floor joist bay is routine in these houses, and the framing is modern, so the budget goes into tile, glass, fixtures and cabinetry.

Borough homes and hall baths

In Yardley Borough's older homes the challenge is plumbing and space: a cast-iron stack, galvanized supply lines and a bathroom fitted into a former closet. We bring the plumbing forward first, then build a bathroom that suits the age of the house, with mosaic floors, a walk-in shower with a linear drain and a furniture-style vanity.
