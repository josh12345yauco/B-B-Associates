The Newtown Market

A Growing Market With
High Renovation Demand

Newtown Township has experienced significant growth over the past two decades, with a mix of newer construction homes (built 1990–2010) that are now prime candidates for luxury renovation — original kitchens and builder-grade bathrooms that have aged and no longer match the homeowner's taste or the neighborhood's market positioning. Newtown Borough's older stock, meanwhile, presents the kind of historic renovation challenges that B&B specializes in.

Whether you're in the Borough or the Township, B&B's 25 years of Bucks County experience means we understand the housing stock, the permit process, and the homeowners. Every project is fully permitted and overseen by our team from start to finish.
