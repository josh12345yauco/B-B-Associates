Where Bucks County Character
Meets Refined Living

Doylestown Borough is the cultural heart of Bucks County — a walkable, historic community with a genuine sense of place. Its homes range from Victorian-era borough houses to sprawling colonial estates in the surrounding townships. New Hope offers a different kind of architectural richness: riverfront properties, historic stone farmhouses, and artists' retreats. B&B Associates has been building luxury kitchens and bathrooms throughout this area for over two decades.

Our Bucks County Modern Farmhouse Kitchen — with its washed-teal island, extended Carrera backsplash, and driftwood LVP flooring — captures the design sensibility that Doylestown and New Hope homeowners respond to: grounded in the landscape, elevated in every detail.

The Bucks County Market

A Region That Values Authenticity
and Quality Craft

Doylestown and New Hope attract a homeowner who is design-aware, often creative, and deeply invested in the authenticity of their home. This is not a market that responds to cookie-cutter solutions. Bucks County clients tend to have strong opinions about materials, finishes, and craft — and they expect their contractor to match that level of thoughtfulness. B&B Associates has been serving this market for 25 years, building a reputation for the kind of meticulous execution these homeowners demand.

We understand the permit offices in Doylestown Township and Borough, Solebury Township, New Hope Borough, and surrounding Bucks County municipalities. Every project is fully permitted before work begins.
