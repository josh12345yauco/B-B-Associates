The Market

Why Wayne & Villanova
Demand More

Villanova sits in Delaware County on the Main Line, adjacent to Wayne, with home values among the highest in the state. Many Villanova properties date to the early 1900s with substantial square footage, high ceilings, and master suites that can accommodate spa-level renovations. The demand for heated floors, custom steam showers, freestanding soaking tubs, and frameless glass enclosures is higher in this market than almost anywhere else in the region.

We understand this market's architecture, its permit offices, its material suppliers, and its homeowners. That institutional knowledge is built over 25 years of consistent presence on the Main Line.
