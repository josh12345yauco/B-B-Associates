Philadelphia Main Line

Where Architecture Demands Excellence —
and So Do the Homeowners

The Philadelphia Main Line is home to some of Pennsylvania's finest properties — Victorian colonials in Wayne, stone estates in Villanova, Craftsman-style homes in Berwyn, and grand colonials throughout Bryn Mawr and Ardmore. For 25 years, B&B Associates Creations has worked inside these homes, understanding the particular demands of Main Line architecture: high ceilings, plaster walls, period-correct millwork, and homeowners who expect excellence without compromise.

Main Line homeowners are among our most discerning clients. They've seen the difference between a contractor and a craftsman. They come to B&B because our track record — 1,200+ completed projects, a 5.0-star average — speaks to a standard they can trust.

The Main Line Market

Built for the Homes That
Define Pennsylvania Luxury

The Main Line encompasses some of the highest home values in Pennsylvania. Properties in Villanova and Wayne regularly sell above $1.5M. Our average Main Line project investment ranges from $55,000 to $150,000+, reflecting both the scale of the homes and the caliber of materials these homeowners expect.

Many Main Line renovations involve historic home considerations — preserved original millwork, non-standard room proportions, plaster wall systems — areas where B&B's 25 years of regional experience delivers results others can't match.
