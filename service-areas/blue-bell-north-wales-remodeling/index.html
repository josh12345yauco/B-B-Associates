Questions from Blue Bell & North Wales Homeowners

Yes — Blue Bell and Whitpain Township are among our most active service areas. We're headquartered in neighboring Ambler, PA, which means Blue Bell is practically our backyard. We're familiar with every subdivision and housing era in the area, and we have established relationships with the local permit office.
Blue Bell's housing stock skews toward colonial and transitional styles built from the 1970s through the 2000s — homes where the original kitchen is now 20–30 years old and due for a transformation. We've executed everything from bright transitional kitchens with shaker cabinetry and quartz countertops to more dramatic designs with custom painted cabinetry, unlacquered brass hardware, and statement stone. We tailor the design to the home and the homeowner — not to a template.
Our schedule typically books 6–10 weeks out from the consultation date. For projects beginning in spring (the most popular season), we recommend reaching out by January or February to secure your spot. Consultations are always free, and there's no obligation until you sign a contract.
Yes. We handle all permitting in North Wales Borough, Lansdale Borough, Montgomery Township, and surrounding municipalities as part of our standard scope. Permits are included in the project — not billed separately. We pull all permits before work begins and coordinate all required inspections through our team.
Bathroom renovations in the Blue Bell and North Wales area generally range from $20,000 to $85,000+, with master and spa-style baths at the higher end and hall, guest, or powder rooms lower. You receive a fully itemized, line-item estimate before any work begins, and a free in-home consultation is the best way to get an accurate bathroom remodel cost for your space.
Most full kitchen remodels in the Blue Bell and North Wales area fall between $55,000 and $150,000+, depending on cabinetry, countertops, and whether the layout changes structurally. Before any contract is signed, we provide a detailed, line-item estimate that covers every cost. A free in-home consultation is the fastest way to get an accurate kitchen remodel cost for your home.
Plan for roughly 12–20 weeks start to finish for a full kitchen — including design, permitting, and 4–10 weeks of on-site construction — and about 5–7 weeks of construction for most bathrooms. Older homes can add time if wiring, plumbing, or structural conditions surface during demolition, which is why we assess thoroughly before committing to a schedule.
