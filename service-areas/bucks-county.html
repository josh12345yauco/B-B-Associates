We Serve All of Bucks County

From Yardley to New Hope —
Every Corner of Bucks County

Bucks County's housing stock is among the most architecturally diverse in the Philadelphia suburbs. Historic stone farmhouses in Buckingham and New Hope, post-war ranches in Warminster and Levittown, and upscale newer construction in Newtown Township all present unique renovation opportunities — and unique challenges.

B&B Associates has 25+ years of experience working across Bucks County. We manage permits with every Bucks County municipality, know the structural realities of every construction era, and deliver renovations that complement the character of your home.
