About B&B Associates

Our Story

Built on a Promise.
Proven Over 13 Years.

Laurentiu Barbulescu and his son Michael co-founded B&B Associates Creations together as 50/50 partners — united by a single vision: bring honest craftsmanship back to home remodeling. No cutting corners. No disappearing contractors. No excuses.

After a decade of building the company side by side, Michael took on majority ownership and became the driving force behind the firm's continued growth across Montgomery County, Bucks County, and the Main Line. Youngest brother Andrei joined as Operations Manager, rounding out a full family leadership team that oversees every project from first design consultation through final walkthrough.

Over 13 years and 1,200+ projects later, we remain a family business at our core. We haven't franchised. We haven't sold out. We've simply continued doing what we do best: building flawless kitchens and bathrooms for clients who appreciate the difference.

"Every B&B Associates Creations project, from initial consultation to final walkthrough, reflects our name. From concept to completion, we bring an unmatched level of care to every project — because for us, this isn't just work. It's legacy."

— Michael Barbulescu, Owner
