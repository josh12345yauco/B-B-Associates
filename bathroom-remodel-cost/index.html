The Short Answer

Bathroom Remodel Cost

In 2026, a bathroom remodel by B&B Associates in Montgomery County, Bucks County or on the Main Line runs from about $20,000 for a hall bath refresh to $75,000 and beyond for a full primary suite with spa features. Most of our bathrooms land between $40,000 and $75,000: an expanded walk-in tile shower, custom vanity, frameless glass and heated floors. Every estimate is itemized before work starts, and the in-home consultation is free.

What Actually Drives the Price

Plumbing that moves. Keeping the toilet, shower and vanity where they are is the single biggest cost control in a bathroom. Relocating a drain in a slab or a second-floor joist bay, or absorbing a closet to enlarge a primary bath the way we did in Yardley, is what pushes a project from Premium to Signature.

Tile is second, and it is where a bathroom is won or lost. Large-format porcelain, natural stone, mosaic accents, curbless showers and custom niches all take more layout, more waterproofing and more skilled hours than a standard tub surround. Our own crews set every surface, which is why homeowners searching for a bathroom remodeler with strong tile reviews end up calling us.

Third is glass and fixtures. Frameless enclosures, thermostatic valves, wall-hung toilets and heated floors are each a few thousand dollars, and together they define the difference between a nice bathroom and one that feels like a hotel.

About One-Day Bathroom Remodels

Searches for one-day bathroom remodel cost are rising across Pennsylvania. The honest answer: a one-day bathroom is an acrylic liner or overlay fitted over your existing tub and walls. It is fast and it is cheaper up front, and it is not a remodel. Nothing behind the wall is inspected or fixed, the shape of the space does not change, and when the liner fails or the substrate behind it does, the real remodel still has to happen.

A tiled B&B bathroom takes 5–7 weeks of construction and is built to be the last time you touch that room for twenty years. We think that is the better math, and we will say so in the consultation.

What Is Included in a B&B Estimate

Design, permits, materials, plumbing, electrical, tile, glass, fixtures and project management under one contract. We file permits with your municipality, whether that is Upper Dublin, Lower Merion, Newtown Township or Doylestown Borough, and we coordinate every inspection.

Vanities, tile, stone and fixtures come through our 60+ supplier partners at trade pricing, typically 5–35% below retail, already reflected in the number you see. Anything we cannot see before demolition, such as rot under a tub or corroded galvanized supply lines, is shown to you and priced separately before we proceed.

How Long a Bathroom Remodel Takes

Most bathrooms are 5–7 weeks of construction after 2–4 weeks of design and selections and 2–4 weeks of permitting. Custom glass is measured after tile is set and typically takes two weeks to arrive, so the last item installed is usually the shower door. Primary suites with structural change run longer. Older homes in Ambler, Gladwyne and Bryn Mawr can add time if plumbing has to be brought to code once walls open.
