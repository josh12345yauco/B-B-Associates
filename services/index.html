Common Questions

Answers for Homeowners

Kitchen remodels in Montgomery County typically range from $35,000 to $150,000+ depending on scope, materials, and structural changes. Mid-range renovations in Ambler, Blue Bell, and North Wales average $65,000–$90,000 for a complete transformation. We provide detailed, itemized estimates so you know exactly where every dollar goes before we begin.
Design-build means one company handles both the design and construction of your project. Rather than hiring a separate architect or interior designer who then hands off to a contractor, B&B manages everything in-house. The result: tighter communication, faster timelines, clear accountability, and a final product where design intent and execution are perfectly aligned.
Most bathroom renovations take 3–6 weeks depending on scope, material lead times, and permit requirements. A powder room refresh may complete in 2 weeks, while a full master bath transformation with custom tile work typically runs 5–7 weeks. We provide a detailed project timeline before work begins and update you throughout the process.
Yes — completely. B&B manages all permit applications, municipal inspections, and code compliance across every township we serve in Montgomery County, Bucks County, and the Main Line. You never have to worry about paperwork or inspections. We handle it all as part of the project.
