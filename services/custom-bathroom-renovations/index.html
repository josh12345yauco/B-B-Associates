Your Master Bath. Reimagined as a Personal Sanctuary.

Award Best of Houzz 2021

The master bathroom is no longer just a utility room — it is the private retreat at the center of your daily life. At B&B Associates Creations, we design and build master baths that function as true sanctuaries: spaces where the pressures of the day dissolve the moment you walk through the door. Custom vanities with integrated storage, radiant heated floors, and curbless walk-in showers are just the beginning.

Our designers work from your vision and your existing footprint to create a layout that feels expansive, intentional, and uniquely yours. Whether the style calls for a frameless glass enclosure with Italian porcelain tile, a freestanding soaking tub beneath a skylight, or a dual-head rainfall shower with a linear drain, our licensed build team executes every detail with precision. We have completed over 600 bathrooms across Montgomery County, Bucks County, and the Main Line — and every single one reflects the same commitment to flawless craftsmanship.

In-Home Consultation

Common Questions

Bathroom Renovation FAQs

A standard master bath renovation with B&B takes 4–8 weeks from demo to final walkthrough. Projects involving shower expansions, plumbing relocations, or heated floor installations typically run 6–8 weeks. We provide a confirmed timeline before work begins, and our dedicated crew model — the same team every day — protects that schedule.
Yes, most clients remain in their homes throughout a bathroom renovation. If the project involves your only full bathroom, we work with you to ensure an alternative is available and minimize the disruption window. We protect the work area daily and coordinate access considerately.
A remodel typically refers to updating surfaces — new tile, new fixtures, new paint — while keeping the layout the same. A renovation is more comprehensive: it may involve moving plumbing, expanding the shower footprint, restructuring the layout, or adding radiant heat. B&B specializes in full renovations — we transform the space, not just resurface it.
Yes, radiant floor heating is one of our most popular additions, especially for master bathrooms in the Philadelphia region. We install electric mat systems under tile, connected to a programmable wall thermostat. It's a relatively modest add-on cost with enormous daily impact on comfort.
We source from 60+ supplier partners including MSI, Porcelanosa, Dal-Tile, American Olean, and numerous regional specialty importers. We also have relationships with custom vanity makers and frameless glass specialists. Through our trade pricing, you access these materials at 5–35% below retail.
Our crew includes licensed plumbers and electricians who are part of the B&B team — not subcontractors we've hired for your job. The same people who frame your shower also run the plumbing. This eliminates coordination delays and ensures everyone is working toward the same design vision.
