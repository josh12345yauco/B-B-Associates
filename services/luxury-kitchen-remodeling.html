Kitchen Remodeling FAQs

A typical luxury kitchen remodel with B&B takes 6–12 weeks from demo to final walkthrough, depending on scope. Smaller projects with limited structural changes can wrap in 5–6 weeks. Larger kitchens involving load-bearing wall removal, custom cabinetry, and complex lighting may run 10–14 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline before work begins and protect it with our dedicated crew model — the same team on your project every day, no delays from crew switching.

We understand the constraints and requirements of historic homes in Wayne, Villanova, Bryn Mawr, and Ardmore, including working within HOA guidelines and preserving architectural character while modernizing function. Our 25 years of experience in this region means we’ve navigated virtually every historic home scenario.
Most clients stay in their homes during a kitchen renovation. We protect the work area daily and coordinate access to minimize disruption. We’ll walk you through realistic expectations at the consultation — including temporary kitchen setups that many clients find perfectly manageable for the duration.
Our network includes 60+ premium supplier partners across cabinetry, countertops, tile, fixtures, lighting, and flooring. We work with brands like Kraft Maid, Dura Supreme, MSI, Cambria, Kohler, Delta, and many regional specialty suppliers. Through our trade relationships, you access these materials at 5–35% below retail pricing.
We handle all permits and inspections as part of every project — it’s included in our scope. We’re fully licensed and insured in Pennsylvania and have established relationships with building departments across Montgomery, Bucks, and Chester counties. You never have to worry about permit logistics.
A design-build firm like B&B handles both design and construction under one roof. You get a unified vision, single accountability, and no miscommunication between a separate architect or designer and a contractor. General contractors typically execute plans they didn’t create.
