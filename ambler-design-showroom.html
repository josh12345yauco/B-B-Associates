What to Expect

A Design Experience Unlike Any Other in the Philadelphia Region

01

See Materials in Person

Walk through curated walls of tile, countertop slabs, cabinetry door styles, and hardware finishes from our 60+ supplier partners. Touch the difference between quartz profiles. Compare cabinetry finishes under real lighting. No more choosing from a 4-inch sample.

02

One-on-One Design Sessions

Meet with a B&B designer in a dedicated consultation suite. Bring your inspiration photos, your floor plan, and your questions. We'll design your kitchen or bathroom around you — in person, not over email.

03

3D Visualization Studio

Walk through your future kitchen or bathroom in immersive 3D before a single wall comes down. Our visualization studio lets you approve material combinations, see the lighting, and experience the space before it exists.
