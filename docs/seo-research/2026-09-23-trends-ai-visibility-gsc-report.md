**Date run:** September 23, 2026 · **Site:** bbassociatesco.com · **Scope:** Google Trends (PA + Philadelphia metro), AI answer visibility (4 engines × 6 towns × 2 prompts), Google Search Console (last 3 months). Read-only; no settings changed anywhere.

**What could not be retrieved (stated up front):**
- **Gemini: 0 of 12 prompts.** gemini.google.com (logged in, Pro model) errored three times on the first prompt in two separate attempts an hour apart ("I seem to be encountering an error", then spinner with no output). Auto-titles on the failed chats ("Kitchen Remodeler Search Error") point to Gemini's search-grounding step failing for this query type. Model picker was not changed per the read-only rule.

Perplexity was fully logged out.
- **Trends:** "tub to shower conversion" and "primary bathroom" average bars had zero height (average rounds to 0) so no tooltip could be read; they are reported as ~0. Town-level Trends data does not exist for 15 of the 16 towns (see 1d).
- **GSC:** rows-per-page could not be switched to 25, so queries were read 10 at a time (rows 1–30 per page). A few clicks on Doylestown/Yardley/Newtown sit on queries below row 30 and are not itemized.

### 1a. Comparison: kitchen remodel · bathroom remodel · kitchen renovation · bathroom renovation · design build

**Average interest (Trends "Average" bars, 0–100 scale):**

| Geo / range | kitchen remodel | bathroom remodel | kitchen renovation | bathroom renovation | design build | Kitchen ÷ Bathroom |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pennsylvania, 5 years | 36 | 57 | 4 | 6 | ~0–1 | 0.63 |
| Pennsylvania, 12 months | 51 | 73 | 11 | 17 | 2 | 0.70 |
| Philadelphia metro, 5 years | 33 | 59 | 3 | 5 | 1 | 0.56 |
| Philadelphia metro, 12 months | 45 | 73 | 8 | 14 | 1 | 0.62 |

- **Bathroom remodel is the bigger term in every geo and range.** Kitchen interest runs at roughly two-thirds of bathroom interest (0.56–0.70). From 60 weekly samples across the PA 5-year chart, the overall means were 38.1 (kitchen) vs 57.5 (bathroom), ratio 0.66.
- **"Remodel" beats "renovation" by 5–10×.** Use "remodel" in titles, H1s and ad copy; "renovation" is a secondary variant.
- **"Design build" is near zero** as a consumer search term in this category. Keep it as positioning language, not as a target keyword.
- Both terms are higher in the last 12 months than their 5-year average (kitchen 36→51, bathroom 57→73 in PA), i.e. demand is up.

## TOP 10 FINDINGS

1. **Bathrooms out-search kitchens in Pennsylvania, and "remodel" is the word.** Bathroom remodel averages 57–73 vs kitchen remodel 33–51 across every geo and range (kitchen ≈ 0.65 × bathroom). "Renovation" runs at one-fifth to one-tenth of "remodel" and "design build" is ~0. Homepage, service pages and ads should lead with "remodel" wording and give bathrooms equal billing with kitchens.

2. **Two demand peaks: January–March and July. Lows: September–December.** Kitchen interest peaks in July (51.5) and January (46.8) and bottoms in October (29.7). Bathroom peaks July (70.2), March (69.2), January (67.0) and bottoms in December (42.4). Content, ad budget and outreach should ramp in December for the New Year window and in June for the July window. You are entering the softest quarter right now.

3. **Cost is the #1 intent.** "kitchen remodel cost" and "bathroom remodel cost" are the top related query for both terms in both windows, with "how much does it cost to remodel a kitchen/bathroom" (+70%/+60%), "average cost of bathroom remodel" (+50%) and "one day bathroom remodel cost" (Breakout) all rising. The site publishes cost ranges but has no dedicated cost/pricing guide pages; that is the largest uncaptured query family.

4. **Provider-intent queries are rising fast:** "kitchen remodeler" +250%, "bathroom remodel company" +120%, "kitchen remodel contractors" +140% (5y), "remodel bathroom near me" +60%, "kitchen remodel philadelphia" +40%, and the related topic "Bucks County" +140% for kitchen remodel. Bucks County demand is growing at the same time B&B is invisible there in AI answers (finding 6).

5. **Custom cabinets is the one rising niche term** (12-month average ~3× the 5-year average in both PA and Philly), backed by "kitchen cabinet remodel" +80% and "hickory kitchen cabinet remodel" Breakout. Walk-in shower is flat, kitchen island is flat-to-down, and tub-to-shower / primary bathroom are too small for Trends to measure. Trends has no town-level data for 15 of your 16 towns (Doylestown only), so Search Console, not Trends, is the tool for town prioritization.

6. **AI visibility is strong near Ambler and zero in Bucks County.** Across 36 retrieved answers B&B was named in 19 and placed #1 in 13, but by town: Ambler 6/6, Horsham 5/6, Blue Bell 4/6, Bryn Mawr 3/6, Newtown 1/6 (as an afterthought), Doylestown 0/6. LBK Design Build (7 mentions), Lang's (5), Sycamore Kitchens, Armitage Interiors and Buckingham Bath own Newtown/Doylestown; Hunter Kitchen & Bath (6) owns Bryn Mawr. Because ChatGPT and AI Mode were signed in and personalized, treat the Ambler/Horsham/Blue Bell wins as an upper bound; the Bucks County zeros are real regardless.

7. **Houzz is the most-cited domain in AI answers, and B&B is not on it.** Houzz was cited 44 times across ChatGPT and Perplexity; Angi 22, HomeAdvisor 15, Thumbtack 12, BBB 8. Every ChatGPT mention of B&B came from bbassociatesco.com plus the HomeAdvisor profile. Google AI Mode ignores websites almost entirely and builds answers from Google Business Profile review counts ("Google +22" chips). Priority order for off-site work: a complete Houzz profile with project photos and reviews, then GBP review velocity (asking for reviews that mention "tile" and the town), then Angi/HomeAdvisor freshness.

8. **The town pages get impressions but almost no clicks.** Flourtown 7,180 impressions / 2 clicks, Doylestown 3,090 / 1, Yardley 2,700 / 3, Newtown 2,600 / 3, all at 0–0.1% CTR with average positions 15–20. Several high-value queries already sit on page 1 with zero clicks: "luxury kitchen design newtown" pos 6.7, "custom kitchens newtown" pos 5.3, "remodel contractors new hope" pos 3.9, "luxury kitchen design new hope" pos 3.8, "kitchen design and build yardley" pos 7.7, "bathroom remodeling contractor near me with reviews?" pos 6.0. Title tags and meta descriptions on these pages are the cheapest lever on the site right now.

9. **Search is already asking AI-style questions, and Google is mis-reading "bath."** Flourtown ranks 6–8 for full-sentence queries like "best bathroom remodeler for tile and flooring near me?" and "what's a good bathroom remodeler near me?", which mirrors the AI-engine prompts and argues for FAQ-style H2s that answer those literally. Separately, the same page is being served for "kitchen remodeling bath pa" and "moore township pa" (Bath, PA, Northampton County); the page should say "bathroom" rather than "bath" where it refers to the room, so it stops attracting wrong-town traffic.
